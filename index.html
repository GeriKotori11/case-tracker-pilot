What the demo does today

A clock on every case, and an email when it runs out

The bar marks 75, 100 and 150 percent of the deadline. At 100 percent the team lead is emailed. At 150 the manager is.

Residents can check progress, with no personal data on the page

The public page shows the type of repair, the block, progress and updates. It never shows names, contact details, descriptions or photos.

It works from a reference number, so there is no account to set up.

Set up for your deadlines

Your pilot is set up with your case types and deadlines, for example complaint stage 1 answered within 10 working days, or damp and mould investigated within 10 working days.

The demo screens above run repairs. The complaint and damp-and-mould clocks are not in them. The pilot is where we set those up with you.
